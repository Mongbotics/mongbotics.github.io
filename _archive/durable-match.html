Robot Identity

Not just a robot. A personality of its own.

Every robot carries a Soul.md, a permanent on-chain record of its identity, personality, and history. As it works, that history grows with it, and it stays who it is, no matter who owns it next.

Identity on MongChain

Every robot's Soul.md is written on the blockchain, not a company server, so its identity, history, and performance can't be edited, deleted, or faked.

What is Soul.md

Soul.md is a robot's permanent digital identity, stored on the blockchain: its ownership history, work, performance, and personality, for life. It's tied to an on-chain token, a Real World Asset (RWA), that proves ownership. Whoever holds the token controls the robot, and when it's sold or transferred, the token, and its Soul, goes with it.
