# Technology page content, and where every line comes from

## Durable site (durable.com/b/mongbotics-v7u36b21/website)
- Robot Identity / "Not just a robot. A personality of its own."
- "Every robot carries a Soul.md, a permanent on-chain record of its identity,
  personality, and history. As it works, that history grows with it, and it
  stays who it is, no matter who owns it next."
- Identity on MongChain / "Every robot's Soul.md is written on the blockchain,
  not a company server, so its identity, history, and performance can't be
  edited, deleted, or faked."
- What is Soul.md / "Soul.md is a robot's permanent digital identity, stored on
  the blockchain: its ownership history, work, performance, and personality,
  for life. It's tied to an on-chain token, a Real World Asset (RWA), that
  proves ownership. Whoever holds the token controls the robot, and when it's
  sold or transferred, the token, and its Soul, goes with it."
- MongChain: "On-chain robot identity and soul. Verifying ownership and history."

## Page layout, as rebuilt 2026-08-27
Hero, intro, then `.soulFile` (the Soul.md record on a dark panel) and
`.ownRule` (the RWA rule as three steps), then the "What is Soul.md" manifesto.

The seven `.soulRow` field names are verbatim from the one-page plan. The short
glosses after each ("who it is", "the work it has done") are the only written
lines on the page, kept plain on purpose.

The "Identity on MongChain" manifesto was dropped: its sentence now sits in the
card footnote instead, so the claim is made once rather than twice. The old
soulCards copy (IDENTITY "Unique by design", OWNERSHIP "The token is the key",
HISTORY "A verifiable record") is no longer used, and is kept here in case it
comes back.
