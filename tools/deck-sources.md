## Slide 9

MongCore: Mongbotics Control SystemUniversal Robot Control - Onboard, monitor, manage, and control supported robots from one application.Real-Time Robot Operations - Give robots instructions, assign tasks, and view their live status, activity, and performance.Connected Robot Identity - Each robot has a profile connected to its on-chain Soul.md, bringing its identity, ownership, history, performance, and personality into the control interface.

## Slide 10

MongChain: Tokenized Robot SoulOn-Chain Robot Identity - Every robot is tokenized as a Real World Asset (RWA), creating a permanent digital identity linked to the physical robot.Verifiable Ownership & Control - The RWA token represents ownership and determines who can access the robotʼs control, data, and management systems.Permanent Robot Soul - Each robotʼs Soul.md stores its ownership history, work and service history, performance, ratings, and personality on the blockchain.

## Slide 11

MongChain: Robots Need SoulPersonality Creates Attachment - R2-D2 and C-3PO are iconic because they had recognizable personalities, behaviors, and character.A Robot Should Build a Life History - Its Soul can accumulate identity, personality, work history, service history, performance.Blockchain Makes That Identity Permanent - Storing the Soul on-chain creates a persistent, verifiable record that stays tied to the robot across owners.

## Slide 12

MongMarket: Marketplace for RobotsA Marketplace for Robot Services - People and businesses can request robots for delivery, transportation, inspection, security, and other real-world tasks.Put Robots to Work - Robot owners can list their robots, receive jobs, and earn income when their robots complete tasks.Grab for Robots - MongMarket connects robot demand with robot owners through a peer-to-peer network where jobs are assigned, completed, and paid for through the platform.

## Slide 13

Why Now?The Autonomous Revolution is HereAutonomous robots are moving toward a future where individuals and businesses can own and operate them as easily as other connected devices. Yet, today, there isn't a digital infrastructure to support this rapid evolution.Ownership Needs a Digital LayerAs robots gain autonomy, mobility, and value, they need persistent identity, verifiable ownership, and portable history. Blockchain provides the infrastructure for that.Robots Can Become It's Own Economic NetworkOnce robots can be securely owned, controlled, and transferred, they can also be shared, hired, and put to work through a global marketplace.

## Slide 14

Business ModelMongCore: OnboardBring robots onto the Mongbotics platform so owners can connect, control, monitor, and manage them through one application.MongChain: TokenizeTokenize each robot as an on-chain Real World Asset, linking ownership, identity, Soul.md, history, and access rights to the physical robot.MongMarket: Take RateConnect robot owners with people and businesses that need robotic services, then take a percentage of each job completed through the marketplace.
